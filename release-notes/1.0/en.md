## English

**Quran Muallem 1.0 – first release**
Free forever: no ads, no subscription, no account. Everything stays on your device.

**The Mushaf**
• The Madani Mushaf in Uthmani script: all 604 pages with 15 lines, laid out like the printed copy
• Swipe to turn pages; jump to any surah, juz or page from the menu

**Recitation & Hifz with on-device AI**
• Recite on the page: words turn green (correct), orange (almost right) or red (missed or misread) as you go
• Hifz mode hides the words – each word you recite correctly appears
• "Show word" reveals the next word whenever you get stuck, as often as you need
• Quran phoneme engine trained on Ḥafṣ recitation; it works offline and your voice is never uploaded
• Pages turn automatically while you recite, and correct and wrong words are counted across pages
• Surah complete: the microphone stops with a soft chime and a vibration
• A light vibration marks a mistake without interrupting you

**Understand your mistakes**
• Tap a red or orange word: see it with full tashkeel and its tajweed (madd, ghunnah, qalqalah …) and hear it in your Sheikh's voice
• My voice profile: add words you said correctly – the app learns your accent and pace; choose strict, normal or lenient
• Listen back to your own recordings

**Listen**
• 10 well-known reciters, verse by verse or page by page, with repeat
• Download surahs for offline listening; playback continues in the background with lock-screen controls

**Plan & track**
• Khatma planner for reading or memorization: daily target, on-track status, juz overview
• Progress: memorized verses, smart review of what is due today, daily streak, week overview
• 114 surah badges: silver for reading the whole surah word for word, gold for reciting all of it from memory

**More**
• Offline tajweed guide (Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim) with search and highlighted examples (Arabic/English)
• Daily adhkar – morning, evening, after prayer, before sleep – with counter
• Offline Qibla compass with distance to the Kaaba
• Ayah of the day; invite friends via WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and more
• 13 languages: Arabic, English, German, French, Turkish, Urdu, Indonesian, Malay, Spanish, Russian, Bengali, Albanian, Bosnian
• Emerald, light and dark theme

**Privacy**
• No account, no tracking, no ads – progress, recordings and settings stay on your phone
• Export or import a backup, or delete all your data at any time

Note: automatic feedback can make mistakes and does not replace a qualified teacher.
