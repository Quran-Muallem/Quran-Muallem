Quran Muallem – Learn Quran with the help of AI.

Quran Muallem helps you read, memorize (Hifz) and revise the Holy Quran every day. It is completely free, with no ads and no subscription – a Sadaqah Jariyah.

THE MADANI MUSHAF, PAGE BY PAGE
• All 604 pages in Uthmani script, laid out exactly like the printed Mushaf
• Surah, Juz and page navigation, and a reading mode without distractions
• Listen to 10 well-known reciters, verse by verse or page by page

TASMĪʿ – RECITE FROM MEMORY
• In memorization mode the words are hidden. Recite, and every word you say correctly turns green
• Mistakes and skipped words are marked so you know exactly where to look again
• Speech recognition runs on your phone. Your voice is never uploaded

HINT WHEN YOU GET STUCK
• Forgot how a verse begins? Tap "Hint" to reveal 1 to 3 words, then carry on from memory

TRACK YOUR HIFZ
• See which verses you have memorized and which need revision
• Smart review tells you what is due today
• Daily streak and an encouraging ayah of the day

TAJWEED GUIDE (OFFLINE)
• Clear rules for Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim: nūn and mīm sākinah, madd, makharij and sifat, qalqalah, heavy and light letters
• Examples with the key letters highlighted
• Instant search in Arabic and English – works without internet
• For Tafsir or religious rulings, please ask qualified scholars

QIBLA COMPASS
• Qibla direction and distance to the Kaaba, also without internet

MADE FOR YOU
• Arabic and English interface
• Emerald and gold, light or dark theme
• Invite family and friends via WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and more

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your progress, settings and recordings are stored only on your device. No account on our servers, no tracking, no ads. You can export a backup or delete all data at any time.

Note: speech recognition is automatic and can make mistakes. It does not replace learning with a qualified teacher.
